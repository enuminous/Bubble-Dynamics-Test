THE IDENTIFIABILITY RESULT

One response. Two interpretations.

The selected memory term gives exactly the same response as a conventional Maxwell element. Even perfect radius and pressure measurements cannot distinguish those interpretations through this observable alone. EFMW’s physical status remains unresolved.

G*(ω) = Gₘ iωτ1 + iωτMaxwell modulus
Hmemory(ω) = HMaxwell(ω)
Recorded relative numerical discrepancy: 5.28 × 10⁻¹⁵. The equivalence is algebraic.

Model & interpretation

LINEAR RESPONSE
What is being calculated?

For x = (R − R₀)/R₀, the linearized equation of an existing spherical bubble is extended by one passive relaxation stress q:

m ẍ + b ẋ + k x + q = −pdrive(t)
τ q̇ + q = 4Gₘτ ẋ

m = ρR₀²; b = 4η; pg0 = P₀ − pv + 2σ/R₀; k = 3κpg0 − 2σ/R₀.

H(ω) = −1k − mω² + ibω + 4Gₘiωτ/(1 + iωτ)

The explorer evaluates this analytic transfer function. The animated bubble shows its sinusoidal steady-state radius response with amplified displacement and slowed time. It is not a cavitation or particle-creation simulation.

What remains untested?
  • The memory kernel is specified for this model; it is not derived from EFMW or TUFT.
  • No Stoney-scale evolution, global driving mechanism, or pressure-regime switch is implemented.
  • The baseline omits full frequency-dependent gas heat diffusion, acoustic radiation, compressibility, and non-spherical motion.
  • Bubble nucleation, collapse, and identification of cavities with matter are outside this linear experiment.
  • An independently derived, quantitatively different EFMW prediction is needed before this observable can identify an EFMW mechanism.
Why does ordinary physics produce memory?

A Maxwell element relaxes stored stress over a time τ. Its complex modulus contributes exactly the same 4G*(ω) to the bubble’s dynamic stiffness. A simple gas-relaxation surrogate can also mimic this pole, although it is not a complete heat-diffusion model.

  • Allen & Roy (2000): viscoelastic bubble dynamics
  • Warnez & Johnsen (2015): relaxation models
  • Brennen: oscillating bubbles and damping

Frozen protocol & verification

NUMERICS: PASS

61 logarithmically spaced frequencies from 2–200 kHz; 31 even-index frequencies for training and 30 odd-index frequencies for held-out interpolation. Each response quadrature has independent Gaussian noise σH = 5 × 10⁻⁸ Pa⁻¹.

  1. Training Δχ² > 4.605608, calibrated from 500 separate null trials.
  2. Held-out weighted RMSE improves by at least 20%.
  3. Memory-model held-out weighted RMSE ≤ 1.5.

No thresholds were revised after seeing the result. All 1,000 trials were retained. Forty-seven of 3,000 optimizer starts did not converge, all under null conditions; every trial had at least one converged memory fit.
